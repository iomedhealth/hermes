## 3. Data Entities & Output Schemas

1. **Enriched Cohort Tables (`cohort_table`)**: Generated by `CohortUtilisation` (`add*` functions) and `CohortCosts` (`addCosts`), preserving 100% of cohort subjects with 0-fill semantics.
2. **Standardised Result Objects (`omopgenerics::summarised_result`)**: Produced by `summariseUtilization()` and `summariseCosts()`.
3. **Economic Study Object (`hermes_study`)**: Multi-stage state object holding CDM references, propensity score matched populations, transition probability matrices, cost distributions, and simulation results.
