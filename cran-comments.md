## Notes for CRAN maintainers
`omopHeor` is an umbrella metapackage providing an end-to-end analytical ecosystem for Real-World Evidence (RWE) and Health Economics and Outcomes Research (HEOR). It unifies 'CohortUtilisation' (Healthcare Resource Utilization), 'CohortCosts' (direct medical expenditures), and 'CohortEconomics' (propensity scores, Markov simulation, and Cost-Effectiveness Analysis) on Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership (OMOP) Common Data Model (CDM) data.
