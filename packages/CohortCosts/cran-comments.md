## Notes for CRAN maintainers
`CohortCosts` links polymorphic Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership (OMOP) COST records to clinical events and tracks medical expenditures, unit cost tariffs, and healthcare price indices for OMOP Common Data Model (CDM) cohorts following DARWIN EU standards.
