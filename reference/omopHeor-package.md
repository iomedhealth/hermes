# omopHeor: Health Economics and Outcomes Research Pipeline on OMOP CDM

An R metapackage providing an end-to-end analytical ecosystem for
Real-World Evidence (RWE) and Health Economics and Outcomes Research
(HEOR). Unifies 'CohortUtilisation' (Healthcare Resource Utilization),
'CohortCosts' (direct medical expenditures), and 'CohortEconomics'
(propensity scores, Markov simulation, and Cost-Effectiveness Analysis)
on Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership (OMOP) Common Data Model
(CDM) data.
